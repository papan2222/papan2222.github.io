river

川が連れてきた命を、ぼくのお腹が受け止める

その重みを、夜がそっと包む

The river carried this life,

and my belly—ever the loyal host—receives it.

The night gently wraps around that weight.

  • loyal /ˈlɔɪəl/：忠実な。
